Choose your pace

Active when you want it. Relaxed when you need it.

Kayaking gives visitors a more hands-on way to explore, while river cruising offers a gentler scenic option. Available routes, water conditions, schedules, and activity formats can change, so ask for current recommendations.

Plan flexibly: local personnel or operators may adjust or cancel activities because of weather, river conditions, safety, capacity, or community requirements.

Before departure

Prepare for a comfortable river experience.

  1. Confirm the right activity. Tell the Tourism Office about your group size, preferred pace, mobility needs, and schedule.
  2. Attend the safety briefing. Follow the operator’s route, boarding, equipment, and emergency instructions.
  3. Protect personal items. Bring drinking water and sun protection, and use appropriate dry storage for valuables when available.

Responsible river travel

Leave the waterway as peaceful as you found it.

  • Use required safety equipment and follow the guide or operator at all times.
  • Keep litter, food packaging, and personal items out of the water.
  • Avoid disturbing wildlife, vegetation, riverbanks, or community spaces.
  • Keep noise reasonable and respect other visitors and residents.
  • Choose local services and follow community-based tourism guidance.

Official assistance

Ask about current river options.

Confirm routes, schedules, fees, equipment, accessibility, group arrangements, and weather guidance.
