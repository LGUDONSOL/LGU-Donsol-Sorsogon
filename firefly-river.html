What to expect

A peaceful end to a Donsol day.

The firefly river experience works well after check-in, after a daytime activity, or as a memorable welcome to town. Conditions, routes, schedules, and availability can change, so confirm current arrangements before setting out.

Nature sets the scene: the number and visibility of fireflies vary with weather, river conditions, season, and the natural environment.

Responsible viewing

Keep the river calm and the experience gentle.

  • Keep voices low and avoid unnecessary noise.
  • Follow your guide's instructions on flash photography and lighting.
  • Do not touch vegetation or disturb wildlife along the river.
  • Keep belongings secure and prevent litter from entering the water.
  • Let guides set the route, viewing distance, and pace.

Plan the evening

Make it part of a comfortable itinerary.

  1. Confirm the tour. Ask about the current meeting point, departure window, fees, inclusions, accessibility, and weather guidance.
  2. Allow enough travel time. Eat or check in before the tour so your group does not need to rush.
  3. Prepare for darkness. Follow operator guidance on clothing, personal belongings, lighting, and boarding safely.

Suggested combinations

Pair the river with your Donsol highlights.

Arrival evening

Check in, enjoy local food, then begin your Donsol stay with a quiet river experience.

After the sea

Rest after a morning wildlife activity, enjoy a relaxed afternoon, and return to nature after sunset.

Slow weekend

Combine fireflies with kayaking, community stops, local food, and time to enjoy the destination without rushing.
