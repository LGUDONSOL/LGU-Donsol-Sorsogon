When to visit

Plan around the whale shark season.

The usual Donsol whale shark interaction season runs from November to early June. Sightings are generally more frequent during the February-to-May peak planning window; November to January is the earlier part of the season, while June is the tail end.

Travel warning: outside the usual season, do not plan a trip around a whale shark interaction unless the Donsol Tourism Office confirms that the activity is currently operating.

What to expect

A real encounter shaped by nature.

Season does not guarantee a sighting. Every Donsol whale shark experience depends on wildlife movement, weather, sea conditions, conservation requirements, and safety decisions. Keep your schedule flexible and treat a sighting as a privilege rather than a promise.

Before traveling: confirm current activity status, registration times, fees, inclusions, and weather guidance directly with the Donsol Tourism Office.

Before entering the water

Prepare in three simple steps.

  1. Register through the official visitor process. Confirm the current procedure before arriving, especially during busy or weather-sensitive periods.
  2. Attend the required briefing. Listen carefully to conservation rules, safety instructions, and the directions of trained tourism personnel and boat crew.
  3. Follow the guide in the water. Keep the required distance and respond immediately when personnel ask the group to move or return to the boat.

Responsible interaction

Respect protects the butanding.

  • Never touch, ride, chase, feed, block, or crowd a whale shark.
  • Keep noise and sudden movement to a minimum.
  • Follow all limits on swimmers, approach, photography, and interaction time.
  • Never pressure guides or crew to ignore conservation or safety rules.
  • Bring reusable water and secure personal belongings to reduce waste.

Official assistance

Confirm before you travel.

Ask whether the whale shark activity is currently operating, especially near the beginning or end of the usual November-to-early-June season, and confirm registration, weather, fees, accessibility, and group arrangements.
