Before booking

Confirm the details that can change.

  • Whale shark activity status—especially near the beginning or end of the usual November-to-early-June season.
  • Registration times, fees, inclusions, and cancellation guidance.
  • Weather, sea and river conditions, and government or safety advisories.
  • Accommodation availability, current rates, amenities, accessibility, and payment terms.
  • Transport schedules and connections from the Daraga–Legazpi area or other parts of Sorsogon.
  • Dietary, mobility, health, communication, or group needs requiring advance coordination.

Season guide: February to May is generally the peak planning window, but sightings are never guaranteed. Outside the usual season, do not build an itinerary around whale sharks unless current activity is confirmed.

Getting to Donsol

Plan the road connection before departure.

Donsol is reached by road from the Daraga–Legazpi area and other parts of Sorsogon. Bus, van, and local transport routes or schedules may change, so confirm the latest connection and allow enough transfer time.

Avoid tight connections: nature activities can move or be cancelled, and transport delays can affect registration or check-in plans.

Suggested stays

Choose an itinerary that leaves room for nature.

Whale shark activities in these sample plans apply only during the usual season and when current operations and conditions allow. At other times, substitute kayaking, river cruising, community visits, local food, or coastal experiences.

One day

Morning: during the usual season, registration, briefing, and whale shark activity if conditions allow; otherwise, choose another local nature activity.

Afternoon: local lunch, pasalubong, and rest.

Evening: river experience before departure or an overnight stay.

Two days

Day 1: arrive, check in, sample local food, enjoy sunset, and join an evening river experience.

Day 2: during the usual season, keep the morning flexible for the butanding; otherwise, enjoy another nature or community experience before lunch and departure.

Three days

Day 1: settle in and enjoy the river.

Day 2: choose a currently operating nature activity, then add coastal scenery, kayaking, or river cruising.

Day 3: local breakfast, market, community products, and relaxed departure.

Responsible visitor checklist

Help keep Donsol welcoming and beautiful.

  • Never touch, chase, feed, block, or crowd wildlife.
  • Keep noise low on river experiences and avoid flash when instructed.
  • Choose local guides and businesses and treat directory listings neutrally.
  • Ask permission before photographing people or community activities.
  • Reduce waste and bring reusable water containers whenever practical.
  • Follow emergency responders, trained guides, crew, and government authorities.
