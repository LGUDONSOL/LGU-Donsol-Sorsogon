Media Credits and Usage Notice

How externally hosted photographs and videos are used on the official Donsol Tourism website.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Third-party media

Photographs and videos on this website are supplied by tourism partners and other third-party contributors. They are loaded from approved external media links rather than stored with the website. Copyright and other rights remain with the respective creators or rights holders unless a specific item is expressly identified as government-owned or openly licensed.

Permission and attribution

The Donsol Tourism Office maintains an internal rights record for each published asset, including its source, rights holder, permitted public-information use, required attribution, approval evidence, and review or expiry date. Where a license or contributor requires a visible credit, that credit must appear beside the media or in an itemized public credit list before publication.

No reuse permission is granted

Appearance on this government website does not place third-party media in the public domain and does not grant visitors permission to download, reproduce, edit, redistribute, or commercially use it. Anyone seeking reuse permission must contact the identified rights holder.

Availability and replacement

Because media is externally hosted, a photograph or video may become unavailable, change, or load slowly because of the provider, network conditions, permission changes, or account restrictions. The Tourism Office may replace or remove media when a link fails, permission expires, ownership is disputed, or the content no longer meets accessibility, accuracy, conservation, or government communication standards.

Rights and attribution concerns

To report incorrect attribution, unauthorized use, or a rights concern, email tourism@donsol.gov.ph. Include the page, a description or screenshot of the item, your relationship to the work, and supporting ownership or authorization information. The Tourism Office may temporarily disable a disputed asset while the concern is reviewed.
