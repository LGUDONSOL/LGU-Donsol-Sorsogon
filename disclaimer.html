Tourism Information Disclaimer

Important conditions for using visitor information published by the Donsol Tourism Office.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Nature, weather, and activity availability

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Activities may be delayed, changed, or cancelled because of weather, sea and river conditions, conservation requirements, safety decisions, operator availability, or government advisories. Always confirm current conditions before traveling.

Rates, schedules, and rules

Rates, operating hours, transport schedules, registration procedures, capacity limits, and local rules may change without immediate notice. The Tourism Office and the responsible activity authority or establishment should be treated as the source for current details.

Business and accommodation listings

Directory listings are provided for public convenience and do not create a ranking, guarantee, booking relationship, or endorsement. Visitors must confirm permits or accreditation where relevant, prices, availability, accessibility, amenities, cancellation terms, and payment arrangements directly with the establishment.

External services and links

Links to maps, social platforms, businesses, and other websites are provided as a convenience. The Municipal Government of Donsol does not control their availability, accessibility, security, privacy practices, or changing content.

Third-party photographs and videos

Photographs and videos are supplied by tourism partners or other third-party contributors and are delivered through external media links. Rights remain with their respective owners unless expressly stated otherwise. Media may be replaced or removed when permission expires, ownership is disputed, the source becomes unavailable, or the material no longer meets government information standards. See the Media Credits and Usage Notice or report a rights concern to the Tourism Office.

Safety and emergencies

Website information is not a substitute for instructions from trained guides, boat crew, emergency responders, health professionals, or government authorities. Follow all on-site briefings and call 911 in an emergency.

Corrections

To report outdated, inaccurate, inaccessible, or rights-sensitive material, email tourism@donsol.gov.ph or use the website feedback form. Include the affected page and the correction source when available.
